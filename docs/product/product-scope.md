# Product scope

## Problem

Koranco Farms currently relies heavily on fragmented paper and manual records. The system will digitize the highest-priority operational workflows while establishing a secure, auditable, maintainable foundation for later expansion.

This is a production system for a commercial pineapple producer/exporter in Ghana. It must support reliable daily work and trustworthy management information rather than imitate a generic administration dashboard.

## Initial scope

- authentication and authorization
- worker register
- field/block register
- worker attendance
- harvest recording
- offline field capture and synchronization
- controlled record corrections and audit history
- operational reporting and management overview
- exports
- administration
- backup and recovery support

## Deferred scope

- inventory management
- payroll automation
- complete crop lifecycle management
- full batch/export traceability
- generic AI assistants
- ML forecasting
- computer vision
- robotic inspection
- sucker-counting automation
- other speculative AI functionality

Deferred items are not implied future commitments. They require separate validation and approval.

## Success philosophy

Success means that confirmed operational work is captured accurately, survives adverse connectivity, remains attributable and correctable without losing history, and produces information Koranco can trust. The system should be secure, usable in field conditions, operable by Koranco, and understandable to future engineers.

Business workflows and definitions remain subject to Koranco validation. Unknown fields, rules, roles, metrics, hierarchies, and retention requirements are tracked in [unresolved requirements](unresolved-requirements.md), not filled in by analogy with other farm or HR products.
